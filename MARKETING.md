# DealSense AI — Go-to-Market Plan: First 1,000 Users

## Who we're targeting first

Solo real estate investors and part-time house-hackers before full-time agents — they have the clearest, most painful problem (manually underwriting deals in a spreadsheet under time pressure) and they congregate in a handful of very findable online communities. Agents are the expansion market once the free tool has proven itself and generated word of mouth.

## Core acquisition loop: the free calculator as a lead magnet

The Deal Analyzer's cash flow/cap rate calculation is genuinely useful standalone. Ship a **no-login "Quick Analyze" page** (`/analyze`) that gives a basic score for free, then gates the full AI summary, red flags, and saved history behind signup. This turns every shared link into a funnel: see value in 10 seconds → sign up to save it → hit the 3/month free limit → upgrade.

## Channels, in priority order

1. **Community seeding (weeks 1–4).** Post genuinely useful (non-promotional) answers in r/realestateinvesting, r/RealEstate, and BiggerPockets forums where someone is asking "should I buy this rental?" — include a DealSense link only when it directly answers the question. Target: 20 high-quality posts/comments, ~150 signups.
2. **BiggerPockets & local REI meetup demos (weeks 2–8).** Most metro areas have a monthly real estate investor meetup (often listed on Meetup.com or BiggerPockets). Offer a 10-minute live demo — "analyze an audience member's actual deal on stage" — in exchange for a mention to the group and a discount code. Target: 10 meetups, ~300 signups.
3. **SEO content + embeddable calculator (weeks 1–12, compounding).** Publish 10-15 posts targeting long-tail, high-intent queries ("how to calculate cap rate," "1% rule real estate calculator," "is this rental property a good deal"). Each embeds the free Quick Analyze widget. This is the slowest channel to start but the cheapest per-user once it ranks.
4. **Referral program.** Give both sides 1 free month of Pro for every referral that completes a paid signup. Surface it prominently in the app after a user's 3rd analysis (their moment of highest perceived value).
5. **Affiliate partnerships with REI YouTubers/course creators.** 20% recurring commission, tracked via a simple referral code on signup. These creators already have audiences primed to want exactly this tool; 3-5 partnerships can outperform paid ads at this stage.
6. **Launch moment: Product Hunt + Indie Hackers.** Time this for around week 6-8, once there are real usage numbers and testimonials to show, not on day one.
7. **Targeted LinkedIn outreach to solo agents (ongoing, lowest priority initially).** Once the investor-side loop is working, use it to fund outreach to agents for the listing-copy and lead-scoring features specifically.

## What we deliberately skip early

Paid search/social ads — CAC is high and unproven before there's a working referral loop and testimonials to make ad creative credible. Revisit once organic channels show >20% week-over-week signup growth for 2+ consecutive weeks.

## Milestones

| Users | Primary driver |
| ----- | -------------- |
| 0 → 100 | Direct community posts + first meetup demos |
| 100 → 400 | Meetups + first SEO posts start ranking + referral loop kicks in |
| 400 → 1,000 | Affiliate partnerships + Product Hunt launch + compounding SEO |

## Metrics to watch weekly

- Signup → first analysis run rate (activation)
- Free → paid conversion rate (target: 3-5% within 30 days)
- Referral program participation rate
- Organic search sessions to `/analyze`
